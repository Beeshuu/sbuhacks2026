      How to survive

      • Bends: a sign on the right shows which way the next bend turns. Bends push the boat toward the outer bank, so steer into one as it starts, or you'll scrape along the bank and slow down.
      • Obstacles: steer into a clear lane. There is always at least one.
        • Floating crate: slows you down.
        • Wave: damages the engine. A question opens straight away.
        • River rock: slows you down and damages the engine. A question opens 3 seconds later.
        • Whirlpool: it drags the boat under. Steer into one and the run is over.
      • The monster: it chases from behind; watch the mirror at the top. It gains whenever you slow down. If it reaches the boat, the run is over.
      • Engine faults: one comes every 750 to 1,500 m, and an "Engine trouble ahead" notice warns you first. The monster waits while you work, but if the timer runs out the engine crawls. A 3-second countdown follows every repair.

        How to fix a circuit
        1. Pick a signal box from the drawer, then choose an empty slot on the board to fit it. You can also drag a box onto a slot.
        2. Choose a slot with nothing picked to take its box out. Press Escape to put a picked box back.
        3. Click an engine to get it ready or send it back to the shed, and watch which tracks clear.
        4. When you think the order is met, run the test cycle. The inspector tries every mix of ready and idle engines.
