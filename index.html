How to play the run
  1. Steer with the left and right arrow keys, A and D, or the arrow buttons at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Bends push the train outward. Steer into a bend as it starts, or you'll run off the track and lose speed.
  3. Rocks and crates block the tracks. Move onto a clear track to get past.
  4. The monster chases from behind. Watch the mirror at the top. Every time you slow down it gains, and if it reaches the train the run is over.
  5. Engine faults stop the train. Fit the right signal boxes and pass the test cycle before the timer runs out. The monster waits while you work, but run out of time and the engine crawls, so it catches up fast.
  6. Press P or Escape to pause.

      How to fix a circuit
      1. Pick a signal box from the drawer, then choose an empty slot on the board to fit it. You can also drag a box onto a slot.
      2. Choose a slot with nothing picked to take its box out. Press Escape to put a picked box back.
      3. Click an engine to get it ready or send it back to the shed, and watch which tracks clear.
      4. When you think the order is met, run the test cycle. The inspector tries every mix of ready and idle engines.
