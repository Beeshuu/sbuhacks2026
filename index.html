    How to play
    1. Pick a valve from the drawer, then choose an empty slot on the board to fit it. You can also drag a valve onto a slot.
    2. Choose a slot with no valve picked to take its valve out. Press Escape to put a picked valve back.
    3. Click a boiler to light or douse it, and watch where the steam goes.
    4. When you think the order is met, run the test cycle. The inspector tries every boiler setting.
