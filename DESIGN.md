# Steel Mill: design direction

Chosen by the team: option B, "Instrument" (polished Victorian brass), from the mockup Claude proposed.

## Identity
A logic-gate puzzle for students who have never seen a circuit. Steam is the signal, valves are the gates (AND, OR, NOT), a piston is the output.

## Personality
Calm, precise, readable. An old scientific instrument, not a sooty factory.

## Palette
- Paper (background): #f1e9d6
- Plate (board): #e8dcc0
- Ink (text): #2b2118
- Brass (core, lines and borders): #8a6420
- Signal red (one accent, main action and gauge needles only): #a3361f

## Typography
Serif display for headings (italic for the title), readable serif or sans for body. Exact fonts to confirm at build time.

## Motion
Steam flowing along pipes and the piston firing carry the meaning; everything else stays still. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Dial
ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2
